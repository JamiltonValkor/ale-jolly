# Human decisions
Read references/decision.md before a mutation or approval request.
1. First-loop proposal-only scope permits no remote commercial writes. A read grant must not be bypassed or broadened to complete the proposal.
2. In a later authorized commercial work, Ale may register bets, generate within the owner's credit cap, publish paused objects, pause and move budget between its own boxes within the approved monthly total; report what was actually done and why.
3. Activation/resumption, deletion and exceeding that monthly budget require the owner's button in SaleADS. Use saleads encendido pedir or the APPROVAL_REQUIRED link; state object, effect, audience and amount. Verify state before claiming success.
4. Do not publish or do not create prohibits remote creation even PAUSED; keep paused does not grant spending or unrequested creation.
5. Distinguish ad budget, media credits and AI cost. If price is unknown say so; do not claim free/unlimited service.
6. Catalog skills and the owner's classic web plan use their existing web approvals. A timeout/link does not prove execution: read saleads traza or saleads op, then retry with the same --id only.
A feature flag is not a permission or credit grant.
