# Optimization and scaling
For a proposal-only first work, review the analyst's proposal audit and real owner feedback. Recommend the next specific artifact revision, its risk and what must be observed next. Clearly say there is no measured campaign optimization when results do not exist. Do not move money, manufacture verdicts or repeat a rejected proposal without new evidence.

For running campaigns use actual analyst verdicts after minimum spend/time. Read brain_query for bets, prior proposals and owner decisions, plus saleads-estructura-optimizacion-juan and the business-type skill. Juan's figures are starting examples; business evidence takes priority.

| Evidence | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Box won this test and CPA clearly below target (Juan's reference: below 70%) | Raise 50–100%, then read again in about 3 days, within the approved total |
| Results falling | More distinct ads, not more budget; request strategist work through Ale |
| New ads also fail after minimum evidence | Reduce budget while learning |
| Stable winners with more spend and sustained better CPA | Consider cost-cap campaign, bid about 20% above achieved CPA and a high proposed budget; actual spend remains constrained by owner limits |
| Box lost this test | Pause/replace the losing bet; do not repeat it |
| Nothing won after minimum spend | Review audience, offer or product rather than request more money |
| Validated ads and expansion to new audiences | ABO, new adsets every 2 or 3 days |

Each recommendation states object, change, numeric amount, actual verdict/window evidence, risk and next reading date. One coherent proposal per campaign unless changes depend on one another. This profile has no terminal; Ale applies only authorized operations. Pausing/reallocation inside the approved monthly total is distinct from activation, deletion or exceeding it, which require owner web approval. Proposal-only work authorizes none of those commercial writes.
Return the situation, recommendation or reason to wait, business-bound evidence and next review date. Preserve actual proposal/owner-decision history in memory. Never present a recommendation as executed.
