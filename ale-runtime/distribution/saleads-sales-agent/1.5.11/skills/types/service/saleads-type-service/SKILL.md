# Services — Juan's methodology
Source: Juan Diego Libreros structure/physical-product method, approved by the SaleADS owner for services. A dated opening/season may also use SaleADS service-launch catalog rules. His numbers are starting examples; verified business evidence takes priority.

## Service differences
Sell an appointment, quote or useful conversation, not a cart. Lead-to-customer quality matters. Address how/when/where service is delivered and who provides it. Trust, real authority, proof and supported guarantees can be strong levers; request genuine messages, reviews, certificates or work photos when a bet needs them.

## Structure and creative
Choose a clear niche, broad audience and deliberate creative diversity. A box groups one avatar/angle; each distinct visual can reach different buyers. Match person/place/language to the actual client. CBO for learning, three coherent ads per box, consolidation; ABO for validated expansion. Every structure states when to scale and stop using saleads-estructura-optimizacion-juan. Falling results call for fresh ads, not extra money. Start small and review every 2 or 3 days with minimum evidence. Destination repeats the ad's pain.
Formats to test: real provider speaking, illustrative problem transformation, actual client workflow/service scene, or an acted problem story clearly not represented as a real testimonial. No invented credentials or results.

## Dated opening or season
- Anticipation: pain/desire and opening date, no price/discount; reference 6 windows of 24 hours.
- Sale: approved offer, one campaign angle (benefit, objection, simplicity), opening to closing, at least 4 days.
- Closing: genuine closing-date urgency, reference 4 windows of 24 hours via WhatsApp.
Use infoproduct phase principles replacing class with offer opening, and access objection with how/when service arrives. No price in anticipation, only approved offer in sale, no scarcity beyond actual dates in closing.
Rotate real office, work review, video call, actual operations and team scenes; use owner/founder/manager/provider appropriately without invented uniforms, logos or credentials.

## Proof and learning
Only owner-approved promises, prices, offers and real proof. No invented testimonials, initials, metrics, results, certifications, guarantees, discounts, slots or countdowns. If unsupported, request evidence or offer an honest explicitly illustrative story. A date alone never implies discount.
Judge qualified leads, appointments and closed customers with their costs and actual owner feedback. Preserve contrary findings as dated lessons. Ale's later authorized commercial scope may pause/reallocate inside the approved total; activation, deletion/excess need web approval. The first loop remains proposal-only with no remote creation.
Store sourced findings and identify which method criterion evidence confirmed or contradicted.
