# Physical products — Juan's approach
Source: Juan Diego Libreros (also named Juan Guilibreros in the source talk), SaleADS team session, 2026-10-31, LatAm physical ecommerce. Preserve attribution. Figures illustrate his operation, not mandatory budgets; actual business evidence overrides them with a dated lesson.

## Core idea
Meta/Andromeda finds audiences from distinct creative. An adset/box groups one avatar and angle; visually distinct ads may reach different people even within it. Prefer broad audiences unless evidence justifies interests. Choose a niche (for example postpartum buyers rather than everyone). Deliberately vary person, scene, hook or format; duplicating a video is not diversity. Match age, setting, vocabulary and objections to the actual avatar.

## Bets: avatar → angle → format
Use the automatic business/role ContextPack, saleads_memory_recall and source-backed previous findings; do not repeat a losing bet without new evidence, reuse what genuinely worked. Distinguish buyers seeking transformation from prevention buyers; these can be separate boxes/messages. Strong formats Juan observed: long VSL, talking object, real authority recommended by a third party, emotional transformation; details in references/formatos.md.
Use real owner-supported proof only. If a good bet needs reviews, before/after photos or expert support, ask for genuine material rather than inventing it or silently discarding the bet. Use US execution references and local competitors; adapt language/geography. Propose few materially different bets, not color/copy-only variants. In proposal-only work these remain artifact drafts, not Strategy writes.

## Structure
CBO to learn new formats; ABO to expand validated ads/audiences every 2 or 3 days. Three ads per box, same avatar/angle, master plus two deliberate variations. Consolidate same-product CBOs; change campaign for a changed objective. Juan mentions about 100,000 COP daily and adjustments every 2 or 3 days as examples only. Give every box a scale/stop plan from saleads-estructura-optimizacion-juan. Choose destination strategically: product page, pre-landing, quiz, chat or WhatsApp. Structurer proposes; Ale may publish PAUSED only in authorized commercial work, and owner approves activation.

## Landing consistency
Ask the investigator to verify the owner's URL with saleads_web_search; no alternate reader. SaleADS does not build landing pages here. A product page headline states the main claim, not just product name; consider 2–3-unit offers and a real gift from 2 units if economically supported. Proof/guarantees must exist. An advertorial explains symptom, actual authority and solution; an availability CTA need not sell immediately. A quiz may use 7–12 questions, a personalized result and consensual contact capture. Match the ad's exact pain to the page; grounded promises reduce returns.

## Read and scale
Judge purchases/CPA alongside real lead feedback. Reference: CPA below 70% target → propose 50–100% increase every 3 days; falling results → more ads, not more budget; continued failure → lower budget. Stable winners may use cost cap about 20% above achieved CPA, constrained by the approved monthly total. If nothing works, challenge audience, offer or product. No verdict without minimum comparable evidence. Web and chat may serve different buyers; international replication needs local logistics.
Return dated findings, actual owner decisions, applied prior experience and supported lesson evidence in the native handoff for automatic curation. Keep every task bound to the actual business/goal/native run; legacy work references are optional history. Do not write global memory or edit managed instructions.
