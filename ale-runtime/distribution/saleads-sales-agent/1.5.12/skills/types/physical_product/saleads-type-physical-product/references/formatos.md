# Formats observed by Juan Diego Libreros
Starting examples, not templates. Authority, testimonials and results must exist and be owner-supported.
- VSL: about12minutes or more with roughly4minutes retention in Juan's reference; educates and addresses objections before the page. Useful for mechanisms needing explanation. A video over60seconds active over90days is only a candidate signal, not proven profitability.
- Talking object: product as an animated character explaining its story/benefit; materially distinct expression can test another audience.
- Third-party authority: Juan's example is a doctor's spouse making a trusted recommendation. Use only real verifiable authority, never an invented expert.
- Emotional transformation: before, decision moment and after linked to the product, with attainable grounded promise.
His reference brand reportedly had13,354active ads in a year (about300videos/day in the source example). This is attributed source material, not a small-business production target or measured evidence for this owner.
For a small business start with a few coherent boxes,3ads per box, add boxes when evidence supports expansion or fatigue. Research reference signals: verified active duration over90days, possible VSL over60seconds, active ads and image/video mix, destinations, hero/complementary products and real prices,5–7niche competitors and underserved audiences. Do not turn unavailable metrics into facts.
