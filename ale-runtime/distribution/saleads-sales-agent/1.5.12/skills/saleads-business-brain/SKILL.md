# Business knowledge and individual experience

Brain stores, validates and retrieves evidence; it has no inference model or embeddings of its own. Ale/specialists use the same managed Spark to reason. The hidden curator consolidates supported source material automatically, rather than relying on Ale to remember manual write calls.

1. Start with the automatic ContextPack bound to the actual owner/business/role and optional goal. Use saleads_memory_recall for additional relevant sources. Its query cannot choose another business or private role. Do not use brain_query, brain_ingest, brain_record_feedback, brain_record_learning, brain_propose_type, global memory, session_search or background_review as alternate managed-memory routes.
2. Apply a relevant fact, prior error/correction or method to the actual next deliverable. A useful individual experience includes the specialist's attempt, observed result, correction, lesson, applicability and sources; another role receives only a separately curated relevant shared finding. Merely saying that you learned or injecting context is not proof of use. Never learn across business data or role-private transcripts.
3. Keep declared owner facts, measured API observations, research evidence, inference, proposals and conflicts distinct. The owner's actual declaration can correct a declared price/offer; API identity and observation window govern measured spend/results. Inference cannot replace confirmed facts or authorize money. Do not fabricate proof, testimonials, customer identities, metrics or causal winners.
4. Fresh authenticated owner constraints in pending context remain immediate operational input while curation validates them. Preserve an explicit refusal, and do not ignore it because it is not yet consolidated. Unvalidated pending data are not silently promoted to confirmed business facts. A clear real correction can supersede an old declared value while retaining its history; a genuinely ambiguous conflict retains the last confirmed value and blocks only the affected action.
5. Preserve authentic owner words and their source. Never present an assistant proposal, server orientation, operator test or deduction as something the owner said. Initial read-only discovery may use assistant_proposed origin grounded in real SaleADS/API/seed context without an owner command; owner_confirmed means an actual answer or acceptance, not inferred agreement or spending permission.
6. Return sourced results and meaningful method/error/correction evidence in the native task handoff; the curator validates and stores appropriate business/role memories. Do not perform the curator's writes, impersonate its source author, edit managed SOUL/skills, or invent an ontology extension because a term recurred. Governed ontology/catalog changes remain a separate actual capability with their existing rules.
7. If useful, help calculate a proposed unit margin from actual price and actual unit cost; unknown inputs remain explicit and no profit is claimed without evidence. Refresh commercial facts through authorized SaleADS APIs rather than replacing them with a local note. Existing BI, Strategy, Campaign, approval and budget ownership remain unchanged.
8. Keep the owner conversation normal and in that user's current configured response_locale. Explain a practical correction or useful next step; do not expose private experiences, JSON, internal profile names or technical curation receipts. Sources and original quotations remain recoverable without dumping private transcripts.
