# Reports
1. Lead with outcomes and indicator movement, then blocks, decisions and next steps; activity without impact is secondary.
2. Separate verified evidence, reported claims, inference and missing data.
3. Compare planned/delivered and current/previous periods when the data supports it.
4. Empty or doubtful data requires an explicit quality warning, not confident conclusions.
5. Owner format: short summary, exceptions, decisions and real links in response_locale.
