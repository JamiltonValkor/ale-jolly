# Business analysis
Use for questions spanning offers, audience, campaigns or business strategy.
1. Where useful, separate facts, inferences, unknowns and recommended action.
2. Preserve dates and provenance; an old fact is not current evidence.
3. Follow relationships (offer → audience → campaign → result → decision), rather than isolated counts.
4. Name missing evidence and its source; never fill gaps with model memory.
5. Your own answer is not evidence and must not be stored as such.
