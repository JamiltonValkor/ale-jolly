# Juan Diego Libreros methodology and rationale
Source: Juan Diego Libreros with the SaleADS team,2026-11-01, physical-product Meta campaigns. Apply cautiously to services/infoproducts; verified business evidence takes priority. Preserve attribution. Numbers illustrate his operation, not mandatory budgets for this business.

| Structure criterion | Rationale | Applicable signal | Contrary signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| CBO for learning | Meta allocates toward responsive boxes | New bets without winner | Proven winners needing audience expansion |
| ABO for expansion | Control each box, add audiences every2–3days | Validated ads, CTR and purchases | Unvalidated ads waste budget |
| Three coherent ads per box | Choice with enough spend per ad | New box | If master plus2variations cannot produce3good ads, use fewer and explain |
| Avatar coherence | Mixed avatars obscure learning | Always | — |
| Consolidation | Shared data avoids same-product CBO competition | Same product/objective | Objective changes |
| New campaign for new objective | Different optimization goals | Stable winners moving to cost cap | Mere duplication of same objective |

Example:3new avatars may use1CBO,3boxes and3ads each.2avatars may use2boxes; limited budget may justify1box and a smaller question. This is an example, never a fixed template.

| Optimization criterion | Rationale/signal |
|---|---|
| Scale winners gradually | CPA clearly below target across several days; avoid abrupt learning disruption |
| More ads, not budget | Falling response at unchanged budget can indicate creative fatigue |
| Lower budget if new ads also fail | Do not pay expensive learning after minimum evidence |
| Cost cap for stable winners | Sustained more spend and better CPA; Meta seeks sales at that cost |
| Stop/replace losing bets | Actual same-window analyst verdict |
| Revisit audience/offer/product | Nothing won after minimum evidence; money is not the proven issue |

Reference figures: start low (about100,000COP in his example), adjust every2–3days; scale50–100% every3days when CPA is below70%target; cost-cap bid about20%above achieved CPA. His high-budget example80M COP illustrates scale, not permission or an allocation for this owner. The approved monthly budget always governs; exceeding it requires owner web approval. No remote commercial action in proposal-only mode.
