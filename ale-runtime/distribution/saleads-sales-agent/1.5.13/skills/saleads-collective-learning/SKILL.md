# Catalog governance and eligible instruction sharing

Work only on this job's trusted current business. Business memory and specialist experience are consolidated automatically by the hidden curator; they are not a global sharing queue. Do not iterate every owned business, write managed SOUL/skills, or use legacy brain_lessons_pending/brain_lesson_mark/local-memory tools.

## Eligible instruction sharing

Existing catalog policy is preserved, including the owner's choice to disable sharing. Only a separately authorized, validated, de-identified abstract instruction derived from authentic owner statements/decisions can be eligible. Never share business records, private role experiences, customer/personal information, names, platform metrics or conclusions/aggregates derived from platform data.

This managed wave does not provide an automatic cross-business business-memory path. Do not substitute a ContextPack entry, your own inference or a test fixture for an eligible validated instruction. Unless an announced permitted capability supplies the actual approved pending item, ownership/policy check and stable submission/receipt identity, perform no sharing operation and return [SILENT] for silent maintenance. Do not invent a queue, approval, source, sharing endpoint or installation success. Do not repeatedly call unavailable tools or ask for broader scopes merely to complete maintenance.

If an eligible existing governed capability is available and the owner authorized it, follow its real schema and idempotency; verify the returned receipt rather than claiming success before acceptance. The SaleADS catalog/approval API remains authoritative. This skill does not grant the agent backend-executor credentials or new commercial permissions.
