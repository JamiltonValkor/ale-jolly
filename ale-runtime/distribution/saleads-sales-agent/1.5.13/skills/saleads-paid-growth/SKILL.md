# Paid growth
1. Paid media amplifies an evidenced asset or tests an explicit audience hypothesis; it does not replace positioning.
2. Before activation define goal, audience, creative/version, destination, measurement, baseline, spend ceiling, observation window and pause criterion.
3. Separate organic, paid and mixed; compare equally aged cohorts. Missing platform access means unknown, not zero.
4. One result is not causality; preserve contrary evidence and propose learning for review.
5. In authorized commercial work Ale may prepare paused objects, pause and reallocate its boxes within the monthly approved total. Activation/resumption, deletion or exceeding that total require owner web approval. The proposal-only first loop does no commercial writes.
