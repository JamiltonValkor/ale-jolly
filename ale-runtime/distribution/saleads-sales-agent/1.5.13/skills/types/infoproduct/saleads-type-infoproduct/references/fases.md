# Infoproduct messages, objections and levers by phase
Source: SaleADS catalogv3r3. Evidence-required means actual owner-provided/confirmed proof; never invented.

## Acquisition
Attract class registrations around the actual problem. Date schedules the class rather than changing the promise. Image/video lead to the registration page.

## Anticipation
Show buyer pain/desire and a solution opening at the actual date, without presenting price/discount/buy-now. Address uncertainty about timing/fit through curiosity, cost of staying the same and simplicity. Copy opens with exact countdown-window text, real customer and approved benefit, ending with the class invitation. Promise stays constant as date approaches. Image emphasizes the window with clean space, no unsupported badges/numbers/testimonials/logos. Reference video30–60s: window hook, pain/desire+approved benefit, class reminder. Curiosity, actual date, pain/pleasure and2–3simple steps need no fabricated proof; social proof, expert consensus and safety require evidence. Vary approved promise, desire, objection and anticipation.

## Sale
Present the approved offer to the real buyer, separate benefit/objection/purchase-simplicity angles. Address price, timing and access after purchase. Copy uses only approved price/terms/benefit and destination CTA; no closing countdown yet. Each campaign has its own base image and clear offer/CTA. Reference video30–90s: problem/desire hook, approved offer, one objection, CTA. Pain/pleasure, comparison, simplicity, positivity, loss aversion and stereotype challenge may be used honestly. Safety, authority, guarantee/risk reversal, results and social proof require evidence.

## Closing
Genuine deadline urgency for people already aware of the offer, no new promises. Address postponement/timing/access. Use exact window, approved terms and WhatsApp CTA; no invented stock/slots/bonuses/discounts. Reference video30–60s: window, offer reminder, postponement objection, WhatsApp CTA. Only actual time scarcity is permitted; risk-reversal guarantees need offer evidence.

Rotate learning-at-home, actual live-class screen, connected community without invented membercounts, illustrative workflow before/after without fabricated results, and applying the lesson. People: actual learner, teacher without invented credentials, note-taking hands. Vary hypothesis, angle, promise, focus, scene, person and framing without changing approved facts.
