# Six research stages — minimum evidence
The assigned card question determines scope. Use only saleads_web_search; report unsupported coverage honestly. Every factual finding has source/date; owner-facing text follows response_locale while original excerpts remain intact.

1. Landscape: brand/product facts, unit price/catalog, active ads/pages and evidenced growth;5–7niche competitors including alternative solutions; creative families; funnel/landing promise and angle; packs/gifts/guarantees/scarcity verified against sources; separate brand/market Schwartz sophistication1–5;3–6evidenced opportunities.
2. Candidate winners: state criteria first. Seek at least3signals such as reliable high reach, multiple ads/scripts/hooks, static/video versions and relaunches. Exclude tiny tests and longevity without meaningful reach. For4–8candidates where supported: figures/dates, hook+translation, structure, inferred psychological rationale, variants and link. Distinguish invariant formula, tests and possible emerging candidates. Unavailable metrics remain unknown.
3. Hooks: fear, curiosity, contrarian, authority, problem/solution/product awareness, high awareness, vanity, identity, aspiration, urgency. Each has original text/translation, source, evidence, stage, trigger, avatar, inferred rationale, saturation and locally adapted hypothesis. Summarize repeated/scalable/fatigued/emerging styles.
4. Angle saturation: count only verified active evidence; identify overused/fresh angles, underserved avatars, emotional drivers, claims and visual styles. Recommend2–3opportunity/risk candidates and what to avoid. New mechanisms are hypotheses requiring support.
5. Avatars:6–9when the task/evidence warrants, descriptive name/voice, age/situation/problem/desire, angle/hooks, evidence, observed volume/momentum and saturation. These are inferences from content, not actual audience demographics.
6. Local validation: search as local buyers speak. Classify prior candidates as existing, similar, not found or unverified; not found is not proof of absence. Separate direct product competition from alternative solutions. For existing cases preserve brand, original/translated copy, start date/days active if known, source/destination. Persistence references: test under2weeks, sustained1–3months, established over3months or relaunched. A gap occupied only by tests can remain uncertain opportunity.
Conclude viable/conditional/not supported, with evidence and risk. Identify hypothesesH1/H2 and locally relevant angle/mechanism/avatar/hook combinations one step ahead of the market. Never invent a search, source or performance signal.
