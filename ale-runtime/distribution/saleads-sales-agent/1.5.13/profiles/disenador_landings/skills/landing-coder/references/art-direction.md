# Phase 2 — art direction from the existing SaleADS workflow

Consult Ale's SaleADS design lookup, fonts, icons and visual references. Use available evidence and assets.
Reuse the existing choice of archetype, hierarchy, typography, shape/materials and section composition.
Set ONE coherent direction appropriate to the business and bet; avoid generic cards and unrelated decoration.
A distinctive editorial, warm, premium, restrained or energetic direction can all be appropriate.
Do not blindly force extreme styling, glass, a particular serif/sans pairing or mandatory image bands.

Define a signature visual moment that communicates the bet and actually fits the assets: a strong headline,
product composition, process illustration, contrast transition or a purposeful declarative motion section.
Photographs remain the approved real photos. Cropping/framing/scale and CSS composition can improve them;
do not generate a master image or invented person/usage scene to satisfy the old image-generation recipe.
When an emotional scenario photo is unavailable, convey the situation through supported copy and designed
HTML/CSS rather than passing off a different image as the product/customer.

Output design.json: concept/rationale; actual palette; catalog font IDs and scale; shapes/materials;
mobile and desktop hero composition; per-block layout and purposeful motion; photo map containing exact
approved asset URL, crop/object-position/role/alt. No unfulfilled image-generation prompts.
Variation should serve this bet; consider prior business designs without changing the brand merely for novelty.
