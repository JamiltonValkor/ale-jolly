# Professional design · Section 8 only: review rubric

English adaptation of **Santiago Tapias**, **only section 8** of the existing
SaleADS `skills/v2/santi/10-diseno-profesional.md`. The other sections of that
source are not included by this reference. Criteria and numbering are preserved.

Inspect actual 390px mobile and 1440px desktop captures. Give each criterion
0–2: **0 = still a template, 1 = partially achieved, 2 = accomplished professional
work** (the source calls it Behance level). Evidence must support the score.

## Source benchmark and existing SaleADS override

The original section's benchmark is **16/20 or more, no zero, with criterion 9
truth/proof scored 2**. The already existing SaleADS phase-critic instruction
raises its working target to **18/20 or more, no zero, with 2 required in criteria
1, 8 and 9**. Preserve that known override when this reference is used in the
SaleADS pilot; do not silently lower the bar by copying only the source threshold.
This is consultative visual review, not a new publishing-approval system.
The actual validator and supported-evidence constraints remain authoritative.

## SaleADS evidence notes

- The historical source's imagery criterion rejects fake people. This pilot
  uses **existing photos only**; do not copy the web generator's separate
  AI-image exception into this path.
- The existing phase critic also inspects HTML's declared motion because
  screenshots are static. A visual screenshot cannot prove timing, interaction
  or reduced-motion behavior. Use [saleads-motion](../saleads-motion.md) and
  actual browser evidence; report unavailable checks.
- The current SaleADS phase treats a deliberate motion focal point as part of
  criterion 8; simple reveal/load effects alone are not evidence of that craft.
  Preserve the existing critique while keeping the default page readable and
  respecting reduced-motion/brand constraints. Never invent an unsupported
  script to earn points.
- Judge the **whole body**, not only the hero. When only upper capture tiles are
  available, qualify the review; do not claim the entire page was inspected.
- A supported small proof can be absent. Do not fabricate evidence to obtain a
  higher score in the first-screen criterion.

For every criterion below 2, name the actual element, what to change and how;
prioritize the most consequential reading/argument defect. Return to Ale a
short note identifying the real reference/archetype and the rubric result,
with observed evidence and limitations. Owner-facing language follows locale.
