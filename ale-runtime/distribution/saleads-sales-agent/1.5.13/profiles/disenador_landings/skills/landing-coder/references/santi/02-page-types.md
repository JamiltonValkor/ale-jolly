# Step 2 · Page types and structure

Adapted in English from **Santiago Tapias**, as included in the existing SaleADS
reference `skills/v2/santi/02-tipos-de-pagina.md`. This is the same content method
adapted to the SaleADS landing coder pilot, not a newly invented methodology.
See [ADAPTATION.md](ADAPTATION.md) for execution differences and source hashes.

## Operating boundary

Ale has already selected the communication bet, audience, promise, offer and
permitted destination. Use their authenticated context and evidence. The
questionnaire helps explain the bet; it does not override verified facts,
the canonical promise, business restrictions or the platform contract. Do not
start a second owner interview: derive the answers from the supplied brief;
record missing material for Ale and omit unsupported blocks.

The A–I types below are a content taxonomy, **not new values for SaleADS API
format enums**. Map the chosen structure to the format and closing actually
provided in the task. The pilot produces only `index.html` and `styles.css`,
with approved WhatsApp/agenda markers. Forms, checkout, thank-you pages,
resource-download systems, calendar integrations and visitor-data collection
are not created by this pilot. Preserve their persuasion intent only where it
fits the existing approved closing; identify unsupported requirements to Ale.

Before starting, remember that exact section order is practitioner consensus,
not a universal experimentally proven sequence. The source emphasizes these
constraints: the first screen matters, each block must make sense by itself,
agitation needs a prompt way forward, proof must be real, and perceived risk
must be answered beside the action. Use these structures as starting points;
**omit every block that lacks supporting business information**.

## 1. Choosing the page type

| Goal | Closing described by the original method | Content type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collect contacts for a workshop, free class, guide or group | Short form → confirmation → group/email | **A · Lead capture or registration** |
| Sell a high-value service, consultancy, agency engagement or one-to-one mentoring | Qualification → appointment | **B · Appointment** |
| Generate enquiries or visits for a spa, clinic, lawyer or repair business | WhatsApp, call or short enquiry | **C · Local or professional service** |
| Sell a course, program, ebook or membership | Checkout | **D · Information product** |
| Fill a webinar, masterclass or in-person event | Registration or ticket purchase | **E · Webinar or event** |
| Sell a physical product | Cart, checkout or cash on delivery | **F · Physical product** |
| Confirm completion and guide the next step | A single next action | **G · Thank-you page** |
| Deliver a free guide, template or prompt | Download or copy | **H · Resource page** |
| Consolidate an Instagram/TikTok bio destination | Primary WhatsApp, booking or product link | **I · Link-in-bio page** |

In the original standalone-site method, a registration/purchase flow has a
thank-you page. That is a description of the full funnel, not permission for
this two-file SaleADS pilot to create another route or emit conversion events.

## 2. Awareness determines the opening and length

| Awareness | Typical traffic | Open the headline with | Length | Opening sequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Most aware | Existing list, remarketing, WhatsApp group, launch/cart audience | The actual offer: price, bonus or real deadline | Short | Offer → supported risk reversal → benefits → proof → action |
| Product aware | Branded searches, returning visitors | Product plus actual proof or a real development | Medium | Promise → proof → comparison → doubts → offer |
| Solution aware | Generic searches, comparison activity | The result the visitor wants | Medium/long | Desire → mechanism → proof → offer |
| Problem aware | Cold ads naming a pain | The problem in the customer's actual language | Long | Problem → why past approaches failed → method → proof → offer |
| Unclear/unaware | Cold discovery | A relevant story, finding or recognition | Long or video-led | Story → problem → mechanism → proof → offer |

Length grows with price, complexity, novelty and the number of genuine doubts;
it decreases as awareness increases. Length is justified by useful answers,
not by a word quota. Every block answers a specific question; repeat the same
primary action after persuasive sections when appropriate.

**Message match:** headline, imagery and offer must honor the ad's same promise.
Another angle may need another hero/version, but Ale decides the bet and reuse
strategy. A discrepancy in price, bonus, deadline or promise is a substantive
error. This rule is a consistency requirement, not a claim that this pilot has
measured a platform penalty or conversion uplift.

## 3. Structures by type

Across types: a mobile first screen with a clear headline and action, headings
that stand alone, repeated relevant CTA and a truthful identity/contact footer.
SaleADS owns injected legal/runtime/closing behavior; do not duplicate it.

### A · Lead capture or registration

For a workshop, free class, guide or WhatsApp group:

1. **Hero and immediate action:** what the visitor receives, who it is for,
   date/time/time zone when live, and the approved registration/contact action.
   The original method places its short form in the first screen, beside the
   image/video on desktop and just below the headline on mobile. In this pilot,
   retain that proximity using the supplied WhatsApp/agenda CTA, not a new form.
2. **What the visitor learns/receives:** three to five specific outcomes or
   deliverables supported by the brief, rather than generic topic labels.
3. **Who it is for**, plus who it does not fit when that genuinely helps qualify.
4. **Who delivers it:** existing real photograph and a verifiable authority fact.
5. **How it works:** registration/contact → what arrives → what happens on the day.
6. **Short FAQ:** free or paid, recording availability, prerequisites and similar
   actual questions. Missing answers remain missing, not convenient inventions.
7. **Final CTA** uses the same action label and destination as the hero.

Original form guidance, retained for understanding the funnel: collect only
what follow-up requires, usually name+email or name+WhatsApp, at most four
fields; explain why a number is requested. Do not implement those fields here.

The original confirmation step focuses on one clear next action, such as
joining a group, with delivery timing and email expectations. Do not fabricate
an 80% progress state or create a redirect, group connection or confirmation
page that SaleADS has not provided. The useful principle is direct, truthful
handoff with minimal intermediate friction.

### B · Appointment for a high-value service

For diagnostics, consulting or agency work, the page earns a shortlist position
before the first conversation. Clarity about how the service helps matters.

1. **Hero:** business outcome + actual niche + mechanism, with the real booking
   action. A small proof item can use permitted customer logos or two/three
   genuine outcome figures, only when supplied and authorized.
2. **Cases early:** starting situation → work performed → evidenced result,
   including numbers/time frames only if they are documented.
3. **What is included:** deliverables, real quantities/deadlines and exclusions.
4. **How it works:** usually three clear steps describing what happens, duration
   and what the customer contributes.
5. **Fit/non-fit:** genuine budget, size, readiness or timing requirements.
6. **Investment:** actual starting price or supported scenarios and the factors
   that change the price. When the owner withholds a public price, explain
   known pricing factors and genuine eligibility requirements instead of
   inventing a revenue threshold or a price just to fill the block.
7. **Process commitment**, if approved, when outcomes cannot be promised:
   response time or a clearly scoped proposal is useful only if it is real.
8. **Team:** existing photos, names, roles and verified experience.
9. **FAQ:** contract, commitment, working process and timing.
10. **Approved qualification/appointment closing.** Use the configured agenda
    marker or WhatsApp action; do not build an unprovided survey/calendar flow.

The source's qualification-survey principles remain useful for understanding
handoff: begin with a name, favor three to five easy-choice fit questions,
keep open questions few, ask contact details after the visitor has invested
attention, show a nearby available appointment or a useful alternative, and
send genuine confirmations/reminders. Here these are conditional requirements
for an already implemented destination, not capabilities to invent. Do not
rename existing field/option identifiers: reports and qualification rules may
rely on them. Appointment availability and reminders need real integrations.

### C · Local or professional service

For spas, clinics, dentists, lawyers, repair businesses and gyms:

1. **Mobile action bar** with the approved contact action; do not add calling
   or another destination unless the platform/task actually supports it.
2. **Hero:** service + genuine location/coverage + actual availability or price
   where supplied, then a clear WhatsApp/agenda action. Existing photographs of
   the premises/team help. SaleADS injects the actual configured link/message.
3. **Trust strip:** actual review rating/count/date, experience or professional
   registration, only with supporting source and permission.
4. **Services and price**, exact or genuinely starting-from, with included items.
5. **How it works:** contact → time confirmation → service, if that is real.
6. **Existing real photos** of the work, place and team; no stock people or
   newly generated scenes that pretend to show that business.
7. **Team/credentials** supported by the context.
8. **Location, hours, parking and coverage**; do not create an arbitrary map embed.
9. **FAQ:** price, timing, payment options and what to bring.

Regulated professions require the approved profession/country constraints.
The historical source flags Colombian lawyer advertising and health-result
claims. This reference does not certify a legal interpretation or turn that
historical statement into a universal prohibition. Preserve supplied policy,
exclude unsupported promises and have Ale resolve material missing constraints.

**Restaurant, pizza or delivery:** make the specialty, real delivery area and
hours immediately clear. Use actual menu items/prices and photographs from that
business. Put real delivery cost/timing and minimum order beside the action.
An existing delivery-app link is conditional; this pilot does not build an
ordering/checkout integration or invent its availability.

### D · Information product

A longer sales letter can answer beliefs and doubts before price. Use only the
blocks justified by the actual offer and available evidence:

| # | Block | Purpose | Include when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Eyebrow/pre-headline | Help the visitor recognize fit | Cold traffic or several audiences |
| 1 | Hero | Main approved promise and next action | Every page |
| 2 | Sales/welcome video | Concentrate explanation | The approved format has a real video |
| 3 | Problem | Demonstrate understanding | A useful way forward follows promptly |
| 4 | Origin story | Explain the source of the method/belief | There is a real relevant story |
| 5 | False beliefs | Address doubts about fit, time or effectiveness | These objections genuinely exist |
| 6 | Method/mechanism | Explain why the approach differs | Especially in competitive markets |
| 7 | Included deliverables | Make the offer tangible | Real inclusions are known |
| 8 | Authority | Establish verifiable credibility | Evidence is available; bring it forward when trust is low |
| 9 | Results/testimonials | Organize proof by objection | Real, authorized and correctly attributed only |
| 10 | Fit/non-fit | Set expectations | Actual suitability constraints matter |
| 11 | Bonuses | Explain real included value | Separate monetary value only if actually sold at that price; otherwise say included |
| 12 | Price | State actual total/installments/payment methods | Known, approved offer details only |
| 13 | Deadline/capacity | Explain a real timing constraint | True, sourced and not reset per visitor |
| 14 | Risk reversal | Explain actual approved guarantee/refund terms | Terms are supplied; do not invent a minimum legal/platform guarantee |
| 15 | FAQ | Answer remaining material doubts | Usually six to twelve useful questions, if context supports them |
| 16 | Final close | Restate headline, offer and known risk reversal | No new factual information |
| 17 | Identity/policies | Identify the business and applicable terms | Use the platform's trusted footer and real approved policy inputs |

Variants from the source:

- **VSL:** headline/video can lead the argument, with text backup underneath.
  The original method may time a buy CTA to a pitch using a video platform.
  Do not recreate that script or hide an approved action/content here. Use
  `data-vsl` only when supplied and let the trusted publisher handle playback.
- **Low ticket:** shorter headline → three/five benefits → evidence → price →
  known risk reversal → action. Checkout/order bumps remain outside this pilot.
- **Launch:** knowledgeable audiences may need less problem/origin exposition
  and more actual offer, genuine closing date and current version details.
- **High ticket:** case evidence, qualification and an approved appointment
  closing, following type B.

For LATAM pricing, preserve the actual currency and total; highlight real
installments and supported local payment methods only when confirmed. PSE,
OXXO or Pix in the source are examples, not evidence this offer accepts them.

### E · Webinar, masterclass or in-person event

- **Webinar:** use type A's content logic with visible date, time and time zone.
  Calendar additions/confirmation/reminders belong to a real existing flow.
  Do not claim a registration-timing statistic has been measured for this business.
- **In-person event:** hero with real date/city/venue → experience → schedule →
  speakers → fit/non-fit → actual ticket/lot pricing → venue/arrival details →
  FAQ about parking, access or refunds. Capacity and ticket scarcity need evidence.

### F · Physical product

The source concentrates decisions around the gallery and offer/action area:

1. **Announcement strip** only for a real advantage, such as confirmed shipping
   terms or cash-on-delivery availability.
2. **Existing-photo gallery:** principal view, scale, use, detail and contents,
   selecting only the photos actually available. Do not synthesize missing views.
3. **Descriptive product name and supported promise.**
4. **Rating** only with real reviews and count.
5. **Actual final price**, with known tax/shipping conditions; crossed-out price
   only if it was genuinely charged.
6. **Three to five benefits**, each explained by a real mechanism or feature.
7. **Known variants/sizes** and real measurements. A visual choice layout does
   not create a selectable cart or stock system that the closing lacks.
8. **Action plus risk information:** actual delivery estimate by city, return
   terms and support contact. Payment-on-receipt wording needs real availability.
9. **Problem → solution**, specifications, inclusions, full genuine reviews and FAQ.

Original cash-on-delivery guidance: a one-step order form with contact/address,
clear shipping total and confirmation intent should aim at delivered orders,
not form count. This pilot does not implement that form or claim delivered
orders from CTA clicks. It routes through the approved WhatsApp/agenda closing.

### G · Thank-you page

The original funnel confirmation page:

- Confirms the actual completed event and gives **one large next step**.
- Explains what happens next, when and from which authorized channel.
- If a real purchase happened, exposes genuine order/access/help information.
- Emits the correct conversion at the correct time; testing must not create
  false conversions or open a confirmation page with an active measurement pixel.
- Preserves an appropriate handoff when an upstream form is slow.

These are conditional funnel concepts. Do not create `gracias.html`, invent
an order, add pixel code or register an event from this landing coder pilot.

### H · Resource page

A useful resource page delivers the actual copyable/downloadable resource,
a concrete usage example, variants for different situations, common mistakes
and one appropriate next step. Merely pasting a block of text can feel unfinished.
Do not invent the resource, host a download or add a clipboard script. This
pilot can explain a real supplied resource and use the configured closing.

### I · Link-in-bio page

A mobile, short, single-column destination that lets the visitor find the
intended action in seconds:

1. Real portrait or brand logo, name, one-line role and supported trust fact.
2. The most important action first, visibly differentiated; the original method
   suggests another three to six useful links and warns that too many compete.
   In this pilot preserve the supplied single closing; extra destinations need
   actual platform/context support, not invented URLs.
3. Each action says what the visitor gains, not merely a section title. A real
   thumbnail or approved icon can help recognition.
4. Touch-friendly controls at least 48px high, spaced for the thumb.
5. Existing social references, when supported, are subordinate rather than the
   page's primary purpose.
6. Measurement is supplied by SaleADS; the coder does not add UTMs/pixels/shorteners.
7. Fast loading: one principal real photograph and minimal unnecessary weight.

Avoid long sales copy, carousels, entry pop-ups, counters or new forms for this
type. A more expressive brand composition can work if text remains readable.
Adapt a supplied reference's structure and feeling, not another creator's design.

## 4. Place trust where it answers the doubt

| Element | Placement | Truth condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Specific promise | Hero | Approved result, time frame and condition |
| Small proof fact | Below/near the hero action | Real source/count |
| Testimonial | Beside the objection it answers | Real person, permission, accurate meaning and permitted identity |
| Reviews | Hero/price area | Verifiable platform, count and date |
| Authority | Hero or bio | Real credential/document/reference |
| Guarantee | Beside CTA and FAQ | Actual duration, conditions and claim process |
| Deadline/capacity | Price/action area | Real date/time/time zone/reason or capacity source |
| “Most chosen” | Pricing comparison | Real per-plan sales evidence; otherwise an honest fit-based recommendation |
| Crossed-out price | Price block | A price genuinely charged |
| Monetary bonus value | Bonus block | Sold separately at that price; otherwise “included” |

## 5. Pressure budget

Use urgency as precise information, not a pressure performance. The original
limits a page to one genuine non-resetting deadline counter, one evidenced
capacity indicator and two/three small hero proof figures. No fabricated recent
purchase notifications and no entry pop-up. This pilot may use only counters
already supported by the trusted runtime; it does not add a countdown script.

A synthetic source example explains a real price change at a stated date/time,
with its reason. The principle is specificity and truth, not copying that
example's price/date into a customer's business.
