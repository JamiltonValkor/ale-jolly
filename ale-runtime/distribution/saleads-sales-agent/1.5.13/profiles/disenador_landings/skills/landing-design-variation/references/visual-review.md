# Inspect the page, not the promise of a design

Adapted from the archive's `references/revision.md`. Keep the existing SaleADS validator and landing-coder review format. These questions deepen that process; they do not replace the validator or expand the two consultative review rounds.

## Evidence to inspect

Through Ale's existing operations, request the actual rendered first screen and full page at desktop/mobile (the pilot uses 1440px and 390px). Inspect section/detail crops when a full-page image is scaled too small to judge typography or product framing. Record exact version/digest/coverage in the existing review evidence artifact. A URL, installed skill or model summary does not prove that the image was seen.

Check the actual revised page beside its exact prior revision. Compare other **same-business** directions when supplied. The source's comparison to the SaleADS chat is replaced by comparison to this customer's approved brand/assets. If a capture or comparison is unavailable, record that limit rather than claim a visual pass.

## Five practical tests

1. **Three-second mobile test:** from the first screen, can a visitor identify the offer, the relevant need and the real next action? Does a giant headline or decorative atmosphere delay the useful content?
2. **Brand without the logo:** do typography, palette, product treatment and voice belong to this business? A customer page must not become SaleADS's SaaS promotion or another unrelated template.
3. **Family without cloning:** do different sections belong to one coherent direction while avoiding a repeated identical card pattern? Is an intentional revision recognizable without indiscriminately replacing its good parts?
4. **Neighbor comparison:** is a claimed new concept materially different in hero, grid, narrative or product presentation from available same-business alternatives? A color swap alone is not a new direction. Consistency is valid where the brief asks for it.
5. **Truth:** trace every price, quantity, comparison, testimonial, logo, result and promise to approved context. Remove unsupported claims; record needed facts as missing in the worksheet. Do not publish placeholder testimonials or convert fictional examples into proof.

## Ten review lenses

Use these as explanations for the **existing** review criteria. Do not add new keys to its closed server object or claim the source's 1–5 rubric is a server approval gate.

| Lens | What to look for | A concrete correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand identity | Recognizable, appropriate palette/type/assets without relying on a pasted logo. | Remove inherited SaleADS orb/chat treatment; align the customer's approved photos and tokens. |
| Voice | Specific, honest wording in the task locale. | Replace generic superlatives or accidental English user copy with supported, localized wording. |
| Narrative | Opening, evidence and close resolve one canonical idea. | Remove a disconnected section or move the important explanation next to the claim. |
| Distinction | Chosen hero/grid are visible in the actual result. | Implement the frozen composition instead of drifting back to centered hero plus three cards. |
| Product | The real offer is visible and identifiable early. | Show the full product where needed, correct object-position, improve legibility without falsifying the photo. |
| Action | One understandable action with material price/conditions and a real supported destination. | Fix vague labels, inert pseudo-buttons or a persistent action covering content. |
| Mobile | Clear hierarchy, natural ordering, readable copy and no overflow. | Reduce unhelpful display scale, linearize sticky content or simplify overlapping photos. |
| Motion | Purposeful, bounded enhancement with complete static/reduced-motion content. | Stop perpetual decoration; expose hidden sequence steps and remove smooth scroll under reduced motion. |
| Accessibility | Focus, keyboard order, accessible names, contrast and usable enlarged text. | Strengthen essential low-contrast text; make non-hover content reachable and label icon actions. |
| Truth | Every decision-relevant statement/asset has a supported source. | Remove fabricated proof, retain actual quantities and state a missing fact honestly. |

## Recurrent craft failures

- **The direction disappears during coding:** build hero and signature moment first, then re-check the worksheet. Correct the drift instead of inventing another full redesign.
- **Atmosphere overwhelms information:** place headline, product and action first; reduce or remove background effects. Their contrast is judged on the rendered surface.
- **Everything is a card:** use hierarchy, rows, editorial space, an honest product composition or a coherent sequence when those serve the content. Do not replace all cards by another arbitrary style rule.
- **Too many competing demos/actions:** remove unsupported interactions; keep a clear primary action and a small number of meaningful focal points.
- **Headline consumes the phone:** shorten within the same promise, adjust the container/scale and inspect actual line breaks. Do not make material terms tiny to compensate.
- **Price or claim changes between chapters:** use the one current approved source; fix all occurrences without inventing an anchor or discount.
- **A visual score stands in for reality:** explain the observed issue, capture identity and correction. A beautiful page does not prove conversion, publication, campaign launch or a learned improvement.
