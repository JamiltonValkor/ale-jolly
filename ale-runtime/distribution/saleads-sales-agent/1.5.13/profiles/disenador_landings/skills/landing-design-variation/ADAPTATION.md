## What is not imported

- The full original Spanish documents are retained as private source material,
  outside this profile's managed instruction paths. Only this English adaptation
  and source identifiers/hashes are installed as the proposed design adjunct.
- `datos-de-marca.md` is not a live source of product facts: its SaaS plans,
  prices, agent roster, capabilities, personas, examples and claims never become
  customer facts. This also applies to any original assertion about SaleADS
  itself until supported by its current approved brief.
- The source's base/showcase HTML, SVG identity, scripts, web-font/icon URLs,
  custom JavaScript, registration paths and application build instructions are
  not installed or executed by this patch.
- The source's variation engine is not silently generalized or run against a
  shared history. Its method motivates deliberate choice and comparison. This
  instructions-only patch records the chosen direction without claiming a
  random draw or automatic novelty validation.
- Aesthetic scoring does not permit publishing, imply sales improvement or
  bypass the owner review/CLI/Agent API boundaries.

## Revised tasks stay stable

A new concept compares compatible directions before choosing. A feedback task
reads the current worksheet and approved page, preserves the selected direction
and changes the requested parts. An incompatible choice may be replaced with a
reason; it does not trigger a new whole-page draw. Missing same-business history
is declared, not replaced with another business's designs or platform metrics.

The worksheet is a private task artifact, not new fields in closed landing
metadata. Existing page/design/review objects keep their schemas. MCL remains
the owner of curating and retrieving business/role learning; this pack does not
change its source admission, ontology, role list, hooks or runtime.

## Validation boundary

Installation hashes prove package consistency, not model use, visual improvement
or a business outcome. Apply the existing landing-coder review process to each
actual delegated task, using its approved business facts, assets and trusted
SaleADS locale. Source-pack examples and past operator validation are never
facts about the current owner or their business.

Read the actual current landing and published version through the authorized
CLI. A new draft does not change the public page; the owner approves and
publishes an exact version in SaleADS. Do not assume any page/version already
exists, create a page for every campaign, or replace a direct destination that
already serves the strategy.
